DoD demo detection catalogue — third-party data + model attribution
====================================================================

This directory is generated by tools/detection_prep.py from two
upstream sources:

1. NAIP panorama (the imagery the detector runs over)
   -----------------------------------------------------
   Source:  USDA National Agriculture Imagery Program (NAIP), served by
            the USGS National Map (imagery.nationalmap.gov,
            "USGSNAIPImagery" ImageServer).
   Tile:    1 m/px aerial imagery centred at 34.724448 N, -86.639802 W
            (Madison County, Alabama, USA).
   Licence: Public domain (US federal product).
            https://www.usgs.gov/centers/eros/data-citation
            https://www.fsa.usda.gov/programs-and-services/aerial-photography
   Fetcher: tools/naip_fetch.py
   Cite:    USDA Farm Service Agency, National Agriculture Imagery
            Program, downloaded YYYY-MM-DD via USGS National Map.

2. Object detector (Pass A)
   -------------------------
   Model:   YOLOv8n-OBB (oriented bounding boxes, n-tier)
   Weights: Distributed by Ultralytics; trained on DOTAv1
            (Dataset of Object DeTection in Aerial images).
   Licence: Apache-2.0 (Ultralytics YOLO codebase + DOTA-trained weights
            released under the Ultralytics licence terms).
            https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics/blob/main/LICENSE
            https://captain-whu.github.io/DOTA/  (DOTAv1 academic use)
   Note:    Weights are fetched on first run by ultralytics and cached
            under ~/.cache/at-detection-prep/ (gitignored, not
            redistributed).

3. Scenario-overlay synthetic objects (Pass B)
   --------------------------------------------
   Source:  examples/dod_mission/assets/detections/overlay.json
            (hand-authored from examples/dod_mission/scenario.py
            landmarks).
   Licence: Apache-2.0 (this repository).
   Note:    Synthetic objects represent the demo's storyline
            (compound-alpha, compound-bravo decoy, hacked sensors,
            insertion zone). They do not exist at those coordinates in
            real life and must not be interpreted as actual targets.

Generated files in this directory (`catalogue.json`, `crops/*.jpg`)
inherit the most-restrictive parent licence and are intended only for
demo use within this repository.
